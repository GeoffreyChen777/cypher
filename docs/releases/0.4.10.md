# Cypher 0.4.10 — a question is never stuck, and you see what happened to your answer

## Sessions

- **A question left behind by a lost run can always be answered or stopped.**
  When Cypher quit while a session was waiting on you (a permission prompt,
  a question), the reply could stay marked as still being written. From then
  on every answer was turned down ("no pending input request"), Stop did
  nothing, and Needs you kept the question forever. Now such a question is
  answered as the session's next turn, and Stop closes it. Chats already
  stuck this way heal by themselves the first time their device starts
  0.4.10.
- **Quitting keeps the session's state.** Cypher now writes a run's end to
  the chat right away on quit, instead of leaving it to a write that a fast
  quit could cut off.

## Answers and Stop

- **You see what the device running the session did with your answer.**
  Needs you, the session's Asking page and the composer used to say
  "Answer sent." whatever happened. Now they say "Sending…" until the
  answer lands, then the question goes away. If the device turned it down,
  they say "No longer waiting" with its reason; Stop is then the way out.
  Stop likewise shows "Stopping…", or "Didn't stop" with the reason.
- The same on the iPhone: a turned-down answer no longer brings the question
  back as "Please answer again".

Cut at build 1 on macOS and Linux, with iOS 0.4.0 (51) for TestFlight. Pi
Runtime stays at 1.5.2.6. A stuck question heals once the device hosting its
session runs 0.4.10.
