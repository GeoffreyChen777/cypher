# Cypher 0.3.66 — Move sessions to another project

## Sessions

- **Move a session to another project, on any device.** A session's menu
  in the sidebar gains Move to Project…, and a project card gains Move
  Sessions to Project… for all of its sessions. Pick any other project of
  your account, on this device or another one, from a palette that shows
  whether its device is online; the move then shows its progress and
  anything that stayed behind.
  - The agent's context, the run history and the transcript's images
    travel with the session, and it continues in the destination
    project's folder. Project files are not copied.
  - A message queued before the move runs in the new folder.
  - Subagent chats move with their parent. Running sessions, sessions with
    a queued message, quick chats and side chats stay where they are.
  - If anything fails before the move completes, the sessions stay where
    they were.
  - A destination device on an older Cypher is refused with a request to
    update it first.

Cut at build 1 on both desktop platforms. Pi Runtime stays at 1.1.0.14
and iOS at 0.2.0 (38).
