# Cypher 0.4.4 — Plans that wait for you

## Workspaces

- **A plan waits for your answer.** When a session a plan started asked
  you something the plan didn't cover, the plan ended as failed on the
  spot, even though the session went on once you answered. The plan now
  stays running, the question shows under **Needs you**, and the work
  continues from your answer.
- **No step spins after its plan ended.** A finished, failed or stopped
  plan closes the steps it left open; before, only Stop did, so a step
  could show a spinner and a growing timer for good. Plans that already
  ended show their steps closed too.
- **A plan that was cut off says so.** If the background session
  carrying a plan out is interrupted mid-answer, the plan fails and
  quotes what it had written. It used to count as done.
- **Plans keep their whole text.** A step's detail was cut to 400
  characters without a word, so the background session could start from
  half a plan. A step now holds 1,500 characters, and the agent is told
  to shorten anything longer instead of having it cut.
- The workspace switcher shows its spinner while a plan runs, not only
  while the workspace's own chat is working.
- Asking a paused workspace to run a routine says it didn't run, and why.
- The workspace's host device needs 0.4.4 for waiting and for whole
  plans. Steps of plans that already ended show closed on any device
  on this release.

## Devices

- **A window survives its engine restarting.** A window attached to a
  background Cypher service kept the old connection after the service
  restarted (an update, a crash): nothing updated again and nothing said
  so. It now reconnects and picks up where the service is.
- **A receive-only device stops dialing.** A device set to receive-only
  can't connect to your other devices, but an open view of another
  device's session made it try about 25 times a minute for as long as
  the view stayed open. It now answers at once with the reason and tries
  nothing.
- A device that is online but can't be reached is retried at a slowing
  pace, as an offline one is, instead of constantly.

## Sync

- **Sessions with many subagents sync again.** With about twenty
  subagents, a session's status row grew past what the server
  accepts, so other devices stopped seeing its status and progress until
  the list shrank, and the refused update was re-sent at every connect
  from then on. The row now carries a shortened list (your own device
  still shows every run), and a refused update is no longer re-sent.

Cut at build 1 on both desktop platforms and as iOS 0.4.0 (45). Pi
Runtime stays at 1.5.2.1.
