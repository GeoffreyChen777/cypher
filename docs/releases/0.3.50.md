# Cypher 0.3.50 — Show only recent sessions in the sidebar

## Sidebar

- **Filter the sidebar by last activity.** The sidebar's view menu has a
  new Last active section: drag the slider (1 hour up to 30 days, then
  All) or type a window into the field beside it, such as `90m`, `18h`,
  `1d 6h` or `2w`. Sessions with no activity inside the window drop out
  of the list, and so do projects left with nothing to show. Sessions
  that are running or waiting for you, pinned sessions, pinned projects
  and projects you just added always stay. The view menu's icon is
  highlighted while a filter is on, and an empty list offers *Show all
  sessions*. The choice is remembered.
- The slider follows the macOS 26 design: the thumb swells into a
  magnifying glass lens while you drag it.

Cut at build 1 on both desktop platforms. Pi Runtime stays at 1.1.0.4 and
iOS at 0.2.0 (29).
