# Cypher 0.3.41 — Scrolling while a reply streams, and translated agent questions

## Chats

- **You can scroll up while a reply streams.** Scrolling up during a
  streaming reply could do nothing at all until the tab was reopened, and
  slow trackpad drags were ignored. Any upward scroll now moves the
  transcript and stops it following the reply.

## Pi Runtime 1.0.1.3

- **Agent questions arrive in your language.** With translation turned on,
  an agent's question card (its question, context and options) is
  translated into your language, and your answer goes back in the agent's:
  a picked option as the option it wrote, typed text translated. The agent's
  own history keeps the question as it asked it. Devices on 0.3.38 or newer
  pick up this Runtime on their own.

Cut at build 1 on both desktop platforms. Pi Runtime moves to 1.0.1.3,
published after the desktop release; iOS stays at 0.2.0 (23).
