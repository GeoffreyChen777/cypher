# Cypher 0.3.63 — Far fewer disk writes, and a session-first iPhone Home

## Performance

- **Saving chats writes only what changed.** Every save rewrote a whole
  chat, and the session list was one file rewritten on every change. A
  signed-in Cypher streaming into a large chat wrote about 4 MB a second,
  and without an account the session list's queue of changes was never
  cleared, so it grew forever: one machine had written 48 GB in two days.
  A chat now saves just the change since its last save, and the session
  list saves only the sessions that changed. In measurements, 120 saves on
  a 6 MB chat went from 1.5 GB written to 14 MB, and 120 session-list
  saves from 146 MB to 2 MB. Existing chats and lists move over on their
  first save.

## Chats

- **Selecting text in the composer stays in the composer.** Double-clicking
  or drag-selecting in the composer selected text in the transcript under
  it instead. The composer, the find bar and the jump-to-bottom button now
  keep presses out of the transcript beneath them.
- **Double-click selects a word, triple-click a line** in text fields, and
  dragging afterwards extends by words or lines. A mention chip selects
  whole.

## Sessions

- **The session rail no longer flickers as it opens.** Mid-animation its
  card shrank and the buttons spilled over the transcript on top of each
  other before snapping back. The card now grows and shrinks smoothly with
  the buttons it shows.

## Progress bar

- **Room for steps found late.** Passed steps never move, so when the agent
  found more work, the new steps piled up in what was left before the
  finish. When the plan grows past its largest size so far, the passed
  steps and the fill now shrink just enough to give each step still to
  come an average step's share. Passed dots glide to their new places.

## iOS 0.2.0 (35)

- **Home lists sessions, not projects.** Every active session is one tap
  from launch, grouped under Now (running or waiting on you), Today,
  Yesterday, Previous 7 Days, Previous 30 Days and Earlier. Each row names
  its project and device.
  - Project chips across the top narrow the list; long-press one to open
    the project's page.
  - Search under the title matches title, last message, project and
    device, including archived sessions.
  - ✎ starts a session in the chosen project, or in another project, a
    quick chat or a new project.
- **Sort and filter sessions like the desktop sidebar.** A toolbar menu
  hides sessions quiet for longer than 1 hour to 30 days, and sorts by new
  activity, name, device or date created. Running sessions and ones
  waiting on you always stay. The choice is saved on the phone.
- **Long turns show their progress bar**, as on the desktop: passed steps
  as dots, a breathing ring for the current one, faint dots for those to
  come. Tap the strip to see the steps.
- **Sessions open faster.** A session opens on its last few messages and
  pages back as you scroll up. A first download of a session's history
  takes a fraction of the CPU it did, and saving it no longer stalls the
  screen.
- **Dark mode is lighter.** Every dark surface sits a step above the old
  near-black, keeping the layers apart. Light mode is unchanged.

Cut at build 1 on both desktop platforms. Pi Runtime stays at 1.1.0.12.
iOS moves to 0.2.0 (35).
