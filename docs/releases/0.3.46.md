# Cypher 0.3.46 — Long Pi turns stay alive, and questions keep their answers

## Chats

- **A long, silent reasoning step is no longer cut off.** Pi can think for
  minutes without writing anything, and the engine took that silence for a
  lost turn and parked it: one xhigh step ran 511 seconds that way. After 10
  seconds of silence Cypher now asks Pi whether it is still working. A busy
  Pi keeps the turn alive; an idle one whose end was lost settles the turn
  with its real outcome. A turn that was parked anyway comes back on the
  normal window as soon as Pi shows any sign of life, instead of being parked
  again after every quiet step, and a question it asks is no longer declined
  on your behalf.
- **Runs Pi starts on its own end cleanly.** A background task waking Pi is
  now tracked and closed like any other turn, rather than left for the
  engine to time out.
- **A question with a time limit closes when its time is up.** When the
  agent gives `ask_user` a timeout, Pi stops waiting at the deadline but never
  said so, and the question stayed open: an answer sent after that was
  accepted and then went nowhere. The question now closes at the deadline,
  and a late answer is turned down instead of lost.
- **Pi is the only agent.** The Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Grok and Hermes
  drivers are gone. Their chats still open and sync; sending in one asks you
  to start a new Pi chat. A settings file still naming one of them can no
  longer leave every agent disabled.

## Sidebar

- **Collapsed groups say what needs you.** A collapsed project or branch
  shows how many sessions are waiting on a question (? n), a spinner while
  any is working, and how many finished unseen (✓ n), in place of the bare
  session count.
- **Only the project you're in floats.** The project holding the selected
  session keeps its raised card; the rest sit flat on the sidebar.
- **Frosted top edge.** The list scrolls under a blurred, fading top edge
  like a native macOS sidebar. The account button moves into the header's
  button row, and collapsing the sidebar glides the window controls into
  place.
- The working spinner is easier to see in light mode, and branch labels
  line up with the session titles.

## iOS 0.2.0 (26)

- **Answering a question is instant.** The question card used to stay on
  screen until the answer had travelled to your computer and back. It now
  closes as soon as you tap Send. If the computer turns the answer down, the
  card comes back and asks you to answer again.
- **Skipping the optional comment keeps your choice.** Tapping Skip on the
  comment step cancelled the whole question, so the agent never got the
  option you had picked. It now sends the option with no comment.
- **Multi-choice questions are checkboxes.** The options used to arrive as
  text to type back, and could hide inside the collapsed context. They are
  now boxes to tick, with their descriptions.
- **Older chats open on the phone.** A chat created before the current sync
  stayed blank on iOS. It now loads like any other.
- **Reconnecting is steadier.** A dropped connection could start two
  reconnect attempts that later replaced each other's sockets.

Cut at build 1 on both desktop platforms. Pi Runtime stays at 1.0.1.4; iOS
moves to 0.2.0 (26).
