# Cypher 0.3.34 — A tiled session workspace, side chats with their own model, and faster streaming to peers

## Workspace

- **Tile sessions side by side.** The window is now the sidebar plus a
  workspace of session tiles. Split it any way you like (presets up to 3×3),
  drag tabs between tiles or onto an edge to split, and the layout is
  remembered per window. Each tile is one session, with its own chat, a right
  dock for Git, Files and side chats, and a bottom terminal. The focused tile
  shows a rail for its terminal, dock, split and zoom actions.

## Chats

- **Side chats can pick their own model.** The model and traits chips in a
  side chat used to be locked to the parent chat's choice. They now open, and
  a pick applies to the side chat's next turn without touching the parent.
  Promoting the side chat to its own tab keeps the pick. Checkout and target
  stay fixed to the parent's working context.

## Sync

- **Other devices keep up with a streaming answer.** A streamed reply used to
  reach other devices long after the host finished, because every small chunk
  was sent separately and queued behind the last one. Chunks produced while a
  send is in flight are now merged into the next send, so peers trail by at
  most about one round trip.

Cut at build 1 on both desktop platforms. Pi Runtime stays at 0.87.1.3; iOS
stays at 0.2.0 (19).
