# Cypher 0.3.37 — Less background traffic

## Sync

- **No more uploading diffs nothing reads.** The engine sent every
  working-tree diff to an old server slot that no client reads any more
  (iOS fetches diffs directly from the host device). That upload is gone.
- **Status checks on other devices are calmer.** Activating the window,
  waking the machine or reconnecting used to re-check every unreachable
  device at once and restart its back-off from 30 seconds. They now only
  make those devices due for a check, keep their back-off, and do so at
  most once every five minutes.

Cut at build 1 on both desktop platforms. Pi Runtime stays at 0.87.1.3; iOS
stays at 0.2.0 (19). The matching iOS change, which keeps live connections
only for the eight most recently opened chats, ships in a separate iOS
build.
